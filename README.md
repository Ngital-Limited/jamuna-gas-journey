# Jamuna Gas Journey

History of Jamuna Gas

Jamuna Spacetech Joint Venture Limited (JSJVL)

Began its operations in the Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) sector in Bangladesh under the brand name Jamuna Gas. The company was incorporated on August 11, 1998, under the Companies Act 1994, and commenced operations in 2000.

Jamuna Gas is one of the largest companies in Bangladesh involved in LPG bulk importation, storage, bottling, marketing, and distribution. It is the first government-approved private LPG plant to operate in this sector. Since its inception, the company has achieved significant growth both internally and externally. It boasts the fastest-growing network of interrelated businesses in the LPG sector.

Safety Tips

Your Safety is in Your Hands. Follow Safety Tips

Cylinder Using

Always keep the cylinder upright:

Never lay it down or place it on its side. Cylinders must always be stored in an upright position.

Store in a well-ventilated area:

Place the cylinder in a space where air can flow freely. Never store it inside cabinets, bathrooms, or any closed space.

Maintain a safe distance from stoves and direct sunlight :

Ensure the cylinder is placed at a safe distance from your stove or any fire source. Do not store the cylinder under the sun or near any heat source.
