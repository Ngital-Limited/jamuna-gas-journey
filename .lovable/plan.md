

# Jamuna Gas — New Website Plan

## Design Direction
Corporate blue and white theme with yellow/orange accents — professional, trustworthy, and clean. Matching the energy industry aesthetic.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Blue (#1A3A5C) — trust and professionalism
- **Accent**: Orange/Yellow (#F59E0B) — energy and warmth
- **Background**: White + light gray sections
- **Text**: Dark navy and gray tones

## Pages & Structure (Multi-page)

### 1. Home Page
- **Navbar**: Logo, nav links (Home, Products, About, Safety, Contact), mobile hamburger menu
- **Hero Section**: Bold headline "Powering Bangladesh's Future", subtitle about being the first govt-approved private LPG plant, CTA button
- **Stats Bar**: 16+ years, 1,500,000+ customers, 3+ Brand Awards (animated counters)
- **Products Overview**: 4 cards — Household, Commercial, Industrial, Auto Gas with icons and brief descriptions
- **Mission/Vision/Objective/Goal Section**: Grid layout with icons
- **Certifications Section**: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001, ISO 45001:2018, WLPGA membership — displayed as badge cards
- **Company Timeline**: Key milestones (2000 start, Brand Awards, 2 Decades Anniversary, Capital Market)
- **Footer**: Company info, quick links, contact details

### 2. Products Page (with sub-sections)
- **Household LPG**: 12kg and 5.5kg cylinder details, applications grid (cooking, apartment reticulation, picnics, hospitality, food court), benefits carousel
- **Commercial LPG**: 35kg and 45kg cylinders, reticulation systems explanation
- **Auto Gas**: Cost-effective, eco-friendly, engine-friendly, safe & reliable features

### 3. About Us Page
- Company history (founded 1998, operations 2000)
- Mission, Vision, Objective, Goal sections
- Timeline with milestones
- Certifications detail

### 4. Safety Tips Page
- Cylinder usage safety guidelines
- Bulk LPG storage safety
- Visual safety tip cards with icons

### 5. Contact Page
- Contact form (name, email, phone, message)
- Company address and phone
- Embedded map placeholder

## Features
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Smooth scroll animations on section reveal
- Mobile hamburger navigation menu
- Clean typography with readable content hierarchy
